# ASR fixtures

The ASR evaluation set (#32): 26 short WAVs (16 kHz mono 16-bit PCM, in Git
LFS) and `manifest.json`, which gives each fixture's category and, for each
utterance, its reference transcript and where its speech starts and ends
(16 kHz sample offsets, `start` inclusive, `end` exclusive).

| Category | Fixtures | Conditions |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `clean-*` | 6 | US English voices at different speaking rates, quiet room tone |
| `cafe-*` | 6 | Background talkers, cups and plates, room noise; 12 to 6 dB SNR |
| `tv-*` | 6 | A TV news presenter across the room; 12 to 6 dB SNR |
| `accented-*` | 8 | Regional and non-native English voices, quiet room tone |

All of it is generated by `scripts/make-asr-fixtures.py` from macOS text to
speech, so no person was recorded and the labels are exact. After
regenerating, re-run `make eval-asr` and update the baseline
([docs/asr-eval.md](../../../../../../docs/asr-eval.md#updating-the-baseline)).

Without `git lfs pull` the WAVs are pointer files: `make eval-asr` stops
with that hint, and the hermetic tests that need the audio are skipped.
